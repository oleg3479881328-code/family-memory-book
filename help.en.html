How to Use This Site

This is a simple guide for relatives. It shows, without technical language, how to open the family tree, find a person, read memoirs, and view photographs or videos.

  • You do not need to install anything.
  • You can safely click around and explore the site.
  • If you are not sure where to begin, start with the family tree and person cards.
Open the Main Page Go to the Steps

Family Tree

Shows people and the family relationships between them.

Memoirs

Want to find a person?

Open the Family Tree section and click the person you are looking for.

Want to read?

Go to the Memoirs section and choose the title of the text you want.

Want to view photos?

Open a person card or go to the Photo Archive section.

Want to watch video?

A video may be inside an album or may open on an external service.

1

How to View the Family Tree

The family tree shows how people are connected: parents, spouses, children, and other relatives.

  • Open the Family Tree section.
  • Click the person you are interested in.
  • A card with details will open.
If the tree looks too large, that is normal. Click a specific person, and the site will make the view easier to understand.
2

What Is Inside a Person Card

A person card gathers the most important information about that person in one place.

  • Name and dates.
  • Parents, spouses or partners, and children.
  • Related texts and memoirs.
  • Photo album and sometimes video.
If you want to view a person's photographs or videos, it is often easiest to do it directly from that person's card.
3

Why the Tree Changes After Closing a Card

This is intentional. It makes it easier to understand the family. After you close a card, the tree refocuses around the selected person.

  • The most important relationships around that person remain visible.
  • It becomes easier to see parents, spouse or partner, and children.
  • This makes it easier to study one person and that branch of the family.
If you want to see more people again, choose another person or click Show All.
4

How to Read Memoirs and Recollections

The Memoirs section contains texts and recollections. They open in a separate window so they are easier to read.

  • Open the Memoirs section.
  • Click the title of the text you want.
  • The text will open in a large separate window.
If a person has related texts, you can use them to go to recollections about that person.
5

How to View Photographs and Videos

Photos and videos are organized into albums. Sometimes a video is stored directly inside the site; sometimes it opens through an external service such as YouTube.

  • Open a person card or the Photo Archive section.
  • Click an album or preview image.
  • If it is a video, it may open as an embedded video or as a link.
If a new tab or separate window opens, that is normal: it means the video is stored on an external service.

Frequently Asked Questions

I am afraid I will break something.

Nothing bad will happen. You can safely view the site, click around, and explore. Ordinary actions will not damage anything.

I do not understand where to click first.

The simplest path is to open the family tree, click a familiar person, and then open that person's card.

Why did the tree look different after I closed a card?

This is not an error. The site intentionally rebuilds the tree around the selected person so that their branch of the family is easier to understand.

Where is the best place to look for photographs?

Either in a specific person's card, or in the Photo Archive section if you simply want to browse albums.

The Easiest Way to Begin

Open the family tree, click a familiar person, look at that person's card, and then go to texts, photographs, or videos. That is the easiest way to understand the site.
